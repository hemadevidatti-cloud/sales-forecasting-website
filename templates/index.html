How SalesCast makes a prediction

In simple words: it learns from your past sales and estimates what may come next.

↗
Trend — the direction

It checks whether sales are generally going up, going down, or staying similar.

◷
Seasonality — repeating patterns

Sales may change in similar ways during certain months or holiday periods.

✦
Promotions — sale events

The website can show which historical dates had promotions. This version does not directly use promotions to change its predictions.

◎
Prediction check

MAE and RMSE show how far predictions were from actual sales in a test part of the history. Smaller errors are generally better.
